final class ReflectedStrategies

Reads the contracts of strategies that live outside the scanned sources.

The bundled strategies of the library are not part of a scanned project, but their contract attributes are readable through reflection without executing any user code. Reflection sees signatures and attributes only, so the body of a create() stays statically unknown and its composition is reported as such, never guessed.
