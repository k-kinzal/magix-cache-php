final class Promise<T>

A typed promise with Guzzle scheduling, adoption and synchronous waiting.

The transport promise never leaves this adapter. Returning a Promise from then adopts its eventual value; callbacks run once through Guzzle's queue. Rejections preserve their original throwable, including programming errors.
