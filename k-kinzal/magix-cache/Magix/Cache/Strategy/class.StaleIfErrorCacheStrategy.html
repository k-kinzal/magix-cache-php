final class StaleIfErrorCacheStrategy
    implements CacheStrategy

Serves a retained expired entry when a delegate fails with accepted behavior.

The capture range is exactly the delegated fetch, and the capture takes only the RuntimeException family — failures the origin declares as behavior. Bugs never enter the catch and can never be answered with stale data; an origin that meets an expected outage in a foreign exception hierarchy translates it into its own declared RuntimeException subtype at the boundary. A served candidate keeps its expired expiration and is never stored again, and extending the physical retention on store never changes the expiration itself.
