final class ExpiresAt

Declares a daily wall-clock expiration contributed by fetch().

On the normal origin path the strategy chooses a future occurrence of the declared local time, or a time in the inclusive at..until window, and meets that finite expiration into the origin metadata. An until earlier than at ends on the following local date; equal endpoints mean a single time. The strategy owns the selection, distribution, rollover, and daylight-saving rules. This attribute neither schedules eviction nor changes execution. Stale answers retain their expired metadata.

These are candidate expirations: dependencies, other strategies and policy TTLs can expire the result sooner. Analysis preserves the local times and timezone without inventing a duration from its own clock.
