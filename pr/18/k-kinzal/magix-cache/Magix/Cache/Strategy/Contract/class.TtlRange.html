final class TtlRange

Describes one inclusive alternative for the finite constraint a strategy adds.

Bounds are seconds relative to the origin base time. A missing bound is undetermined, not a promise of unlimited retention. References are bound in the enclosing Ttl or AssumeTtl declaration's argument environment.
