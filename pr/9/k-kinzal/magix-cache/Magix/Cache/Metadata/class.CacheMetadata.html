final class CacheMetadata

Immutable cache constraints that compose only through the fixed meet law.

The meet is the sole way to add constraints to existing metadata: it selects the earliest finite expiration, combines cacheability with AND, chooses the stricter visibility, and unions tags and diagnostic reasons. top() is its identity, and every composition is as strict as or stricter than each input.
