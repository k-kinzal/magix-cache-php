12 *
13 * A leaf strategy carries the lifetime contract of its fetch operation; a
14 * composition additionally carries the create() parameters, the children it
15 * composes in order, and the explicit assumptions declared for children the
16 * analysis cannot reach. When the composition cannot be read statically the
17 * children stay null with a note, never an empty guess.
